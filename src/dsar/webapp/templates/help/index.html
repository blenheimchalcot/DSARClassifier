How a request goes through

A Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) gives the requester the right to a copy of their personal data. This app takes you from the exported data to the response: every passage that mentions the data subject is scored, you decide what is their personal data, and the app builds the Personal Data Report from your decisions. Everything you decide is recorded under your name in the case audit log.

Finding your way

  • Help in the top bar opens the step for the page you are on.
  • The case page (Current case in the top bar) always shows the Next step and how many days are left.
  • Anything that keeps the report a DRAFT is listed, with a button to clear it, on the Personal Data Reviewer's front page (What's left to do) and on the Report page (Left to process).
