The case page (Current case in the top bar) is the checklist for sending the response. It shows the Next step, the deadline, what the case still needs and the state of every release document.

The deadline

One calendar month from the date the request was received: the same date next month, or that month's last day if it has none. If that falls on a weekend or bank holiday, the next working day. Confirm the date received first: until then the deadline is unknown. An extension of up to two further months is possible for complex or numerous requests, but the requester must be told within the first month, with the reasons. Record it under Extension decided.

From the case

Ticked by the app as the work is done: items decided, passages chosen, no conflicts, every Personal Data Reviewer folder decided, and a final (not draft) Personal Data Report built. Each line links to where the work is.

Release documents

Every template in the Templates folder is a document the response needs. The case page finds each prepared copy in the case's documents folder (DSAR_CASE_DOCS_ROOT/<case>/Output) and shows whether it is missing, an unchanged template, to complete ([placeholders], INSERT guidance or notes still in it) or ready. Every enclosure the letter lists must be there.

DocumentFor
Response letterConfirms the processing, the purposes and the requester's rights, and lists what is enclosed.
Personal Data ReportThe personal data, extracted (step 4).
Exemptions ReportWhat was withheld under the Data Protection Act 2018, and why.
Data not in ScopeWhat was asked for but isn't the requester's personal data, and why.
EnclosuresThe privacy notice, and any documents released in full or redacted.

The page only reads these files. Keep the case folder on the device: OneDrive online-only files aren't opened, because that would download them.

Confirmed by you

Some things only a person can confirm: the requester's identity, the controller, the search scope, open items, each final document and, last, that the response was sent (date, method, and what was sent). Type what you confirmed and press Confirm. It goes in the audit log with your name. Undo adds a new entry; nothing is erased.

Before you send

  • The name on every document is the data subject's, and the controller named is the right legal entity.
  • The letter's description of the search matches what was searched, and anything not searched is explained.
  • No private register, notes, draft or comment goes out. Remove Word comments and document properties, and send PDFs.
  • The DPO has signed off the exemptions and the response.
