Each rule exists because something went wrong without it. The full set, for operators: docs/OPERATIONS.md.

RuleWhy
One case at a time, and check it's the right one. The banner names the data subject; DSAR_DATA_DIR decides the case.One person's data must never end up in another person's response.
Case data stays in the case folder, on this machine, outside OneDrive and git. Don't copy it, email it or upload it.The code folder is synced and the repository is shared.
Never delete. Superseded files are moved to an archive folder, never removed. Corrections are new entries.The case must show what was done, by whom and when.
Never trust the model. A score is a hint. Read the text before you decide.The model has invented quotes and scored passages too high and too low.
Sign in as yourself, and sign out when you hand over.Every decision is recorded under the name signed in.
Build the report in the app. Never by hand or with another tool.Only the app's build is tied to the decisions, the register and the audit log.
Exclude, don't delete. Material that isn't this request's (another request's case files, for example) is labelled and kept out of scope, with the decision logged.It can be most of the hits, and the reason must be on record.
Ask, don't guess, on exemptions and anything unusual: ask the DPO.Withholding needs a lawful reason; releasing someone else's data is a breach.
