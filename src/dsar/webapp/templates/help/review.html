This is where you decide, paragraph by paragraph, what is the data subject's personal data. What you extract goes into the Personal Data Report. What you leave out stays out, with your reason on record. Every decision is saved as you make it, under your name.

Start

  1. On the case page, press Go to review →. Every item scored {{ min_score }} or more that nobody has reviewed yet is brought in, and the first one opens.
  2. Or open Personal Data Reviewer in the top bar. Its front page shows What's left to do, every folder of items, and buttons to bring items into review by score.

Bringing items in copies each item's exact text into a new folder (review_stack_<time>), shown with a plain name such as Items scored 7 (24 Nov). The step is logged.

Decide each paragraph

DecisionKeyWhen
ExtractEThe paragraph is the subject's personal data and can be given to them as it is.
Leave outLNone of it goes in. Pick the reason (numbers 1–9 pick from the list); Shift+L repeats your last reason.
Leave part outPSome of it is the subject's data and some isn't: select the text to cut (for example another person's details), with a reason. The rest is extracted.
Whole itemW / XExtract every undecided paragraph of the item, or leave them all out as not the requester's personal data (it asks first).

Other keys: C opens the Context (the whole document, the whole original item and the conversation around it); ↓ ↑ or J K move; S asks the local model to Suggest score, a hint and never a decision.

Use Duplicate – already in the Personal Data Report for data that is already extracted elsewhere: the report gives each piece of data once. Use Other only with a note, and ask the DPO when you aren't sure an exemption applies. Don't guess.

What is the subject's personal data?

  • Extract what is about them: details that identify them, what was decided or done about them, opinions about them, what they said or wrote.
  • Leave out what only mentions them in passing (a CC line, a meeting invite), business content that isn't about them, and email headers, signatures and quoted threads that carry nothing new.
  • Other people's details are third-party personal data: extract the subject's part and leave the other person's out (Leave part out).
  • Legal advice to or from a lawyer may be privileged: leave it out as Legal professional privilege and tell the DPO.

Repeats and conflicts

Email threads quote each other, so the same paragraph turns up in many files.

  • Repeats: text you have already decided elsewhere. Each repeat sits beside your earlier decision. Confirm match applies the same outcome (an earlier extract becomes Duplicate here); Not a match sends it back to review. Repeats keep the report a DRAFT until confirmed, so clear them; the strip at the top and What's left to do name the folder they're in.
  • Conflicts: the same text extracted in one place and left out in another. The report holds it back until you settle it, side by side in Conflicts.

Sections

To review is what needs your decision. Paragraphs scored below {{ min_score }} fold away: they need no decision and stay out of the report unless you open one and extract it. Reviewed lets you check or change what was decided, filtered by decision or reason. Every change is a new entry: nothing is overwritten.

Done when

What's left to do says Nothing left to review, and the strip at the top shows Build report →.
